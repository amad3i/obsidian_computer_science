# Macbeath region

In mathematics, a MacBeath region is an explicitly defined region in convex analysis on a bounded convex subset of d-dimensional Euclidean space 
  
    
      
        
          
            R
          
          
            d
          
        
      
    
    
  
. The idea was introduced by Alexander Murray MacBeath (1952) and dubbed by G. Ewald, D. G. Larman and C. A. Rogers in 1970. MacBeath regions have been used to solve certain complex problems in the study of the boundaries of convex bodies. Recently they have been used in the study of convex approximations and other aspects of computational geometry.

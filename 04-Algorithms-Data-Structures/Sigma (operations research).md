# Sigma (operations research)

Sigma (Science in General Management) was a limited company established by Henry Novy, Stafford Beer and Roger Eddison in 1961. It sold operational research (OR) in the United Kingdom and overseas. Beer was responsible for the cybernetic models of organisation and corporate planning which were the firm's specially. Eddison was the operations director. It soon became the first substantial Operations Research consultancy firm in the UK. By the time Stafford Beer left in 1966 it employed 50 people.

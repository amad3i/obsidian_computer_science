# Non-local quantum computation

A non-local quantum computation (or NLQC) is a distributed method of performing a quantum computation; the method involves shared  entanglement and a single, simultaneous round of communication. NLQC was initially studied as a cheating strategy in the context of quantum position verification, and has since been related to a number of other subjects including computational complexity, aspects of classical information-theoretic cryptography, and the AdS/CFT correspondence, among other subjects.

# How the Hippies Saved Physics

How the Hippies Saved Physics: Science, Counterculture, and the Quantum Revival is a 2011 book by physicist David Kaiser. The book received the Davis Prize from the History of Science Society in 2013 and was Physics World's 2012 Book of the Year.

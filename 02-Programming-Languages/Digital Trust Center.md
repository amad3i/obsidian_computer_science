# Digital Trust Center

The Digital Trust Center was a Dutch government agency as part of the Ministry of Economic Affairs and Climate Policy (Dutch: Ministerie van Economische Zaken en Klimaat). It was supported by the Confederation of Netherlands Industry and Employers (VNO-NCW), the Royal Association MKB-Nederland), the National Coordinator for Security and Counterterrorism, the National Cyber Security Centre / Platform for the Information Society, Platform Netherlands, NLdigital, and the Dutch Chamber of Commerce.
On January 1 2026, the organization merged into the National Cyber Security Center (NCSC-NL).

# Cloaked

Cloaked is an American consumer privacy and security technology company. The company develops software that lets individuals generate disposable "identities", including masked email addresses, phone numbers, usernames, and passwords. The company later expanded into related areas such as data broker removal, identity-theft insurance, dark-web monitoring, call screening, and a virtual private network.

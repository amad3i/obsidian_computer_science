# NebulaGraph

NebulaGraph is a high-performance, distributed, native graph database built for super large-scale graphs with milliseconds of latency. Designed by the team at Vesoft, it enables fast, efficient, multi-hop searches on graphs with hundreds of billions of nodes and trillions of edges. NebulaGraph adopts the Apache 2.0 license and comes with a wide range of data visualization tools.

# Litecoin

Litecoin (Abbreviation: LTC) is a cryptocurrency and open-source software project. Litecoin was the first cryptocurrency after bitcoin, starting in October 2011.
The Litecoin blockchain is similar to the bitcoin codebase, modified for lower transaction fees. Due to its similarities to Bitcoin, Litecoin has sometimes been referred to as the "silver to Bitcoin's gold."

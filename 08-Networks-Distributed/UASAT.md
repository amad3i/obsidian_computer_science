# UASAT

UASAT is a planned low-earth orbit satellite internet constellation of Ukraine. The project aims to launch 120 satellites into orbit by 2027, with the first satellite launch scheduled for October 2026, and is intended to reduce the country's reliance on foreign constellations such as Starlink.

### Analysis of Memory-Runtime Trade-offs in Caching Strategies for Genetic Programming Symbolic Regression

Genetic Programming Symbolic Regression (GPSR) generates mathematical expressions to model input-output relationships using an evolutionary process. A significant challenge in GPSR lies in the repeated evaluation of entire expressions or their sub-expression, which inflates computational runtime. To address this inefficiency, caching mechanisms have been employed to reduce redundant computations.

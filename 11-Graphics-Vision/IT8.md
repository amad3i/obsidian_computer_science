# IT8

IT8 is a set of American National Standards Institute (ANSI) standards for color communications and control specifications.  Formerly governed by the IT8 Committee, IT8 activities were merged with those of the Committee for Graphics Arts Technologies Standards (CGATS Archived November 9, 2018, at the Wayback Machine) in 1994.

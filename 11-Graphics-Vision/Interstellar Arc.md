# Interstellar Arc

Interstellar Arc is a 60-minute location-based virtual reality (LBVR) experience created by Felix & Paul Studios and installed at Area15 in Las Vegas. The attraction utilizes narrative-driven virtual reality, physical sets, and environmental effects to depict a science-fiction mission of interstellar exploration. It soft-opened in October 2025 and officially launched in November 2025.

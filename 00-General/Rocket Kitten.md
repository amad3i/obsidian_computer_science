# Rocket Kitten

Rocket Kitten or the Rocket Kitten Group is a hacker group thought to be linked to the Iranian government. The threat actor group has targeted a variety of organizations and individuals, particularly in the Middle East, including Israel, Saudi Arabia, Iran, the United States, and the Netherlands.

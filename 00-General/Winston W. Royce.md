# Winston W. Royce

Winston Walker Royce (August 15, 1929 – June 7, 1995) was an American computer scientist and former director at Lockheed Software Technology Center in Austin, Texas. He was a pioneer in the field of software development, known for his 1970 paper from which the Waterfall model was mistakenly drawn.

# Global brain

The global brain is a futurology concept inspired by various neurosciences and pop-culture. The framework for the concept is based off of the planetary information and communications technology (ICT) network, although the theory has evolved outside of discussions related to ICT and its contemporaries. As this network stores more information, and is supplementary relied on for the functions of daily life and communication, it is posited that the global brain will become a form of collective consciousness. See also, Averroes's theory of the unity of the intellect.

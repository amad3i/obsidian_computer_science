# Eduardo Reck Miranda

Eduardo Reck Miranda is a Brazilian computer scientist and composer, known for his research into music with artificial intelligence, brain–computer interfaces, and unconventional computing. He has played a leading role in the development of brain–computer music interfacing (BCMI) and is regarded as a pioneer of quantum computer music, a concept he introduced for research exploring applications of quantum computing in musical composition and performance.

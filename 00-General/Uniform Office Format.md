# Uniform Office Format

Uniform Office Format (UOF; Chinese: 标文通, lit. "standard text general"), sometimes known as Unified Office Format, is an open standard for office applications developed in China. It includes word processing, presentation, and spreadsheet modules, and is made up of GUI, API, and format specifications. The document format described uses XML contained in a compressed file container, similar to OpenDocument and Office Open XML.
The working group that produced the standard was founded in January 2002, and the first draft of the specification was produced in December 2005.

# Lokalise

Lokalise is localization platform designed to help teams adapt digital content for multilingual audiences. Commonly known as a translation management system (TMS), Lokalise has evolved to offer AI translation as a service and plug-and-play automation while serving as a central hub for managing translations across mobile apps, websites, software, games, and other digital products. The company is headquartered remotely and operates globally.

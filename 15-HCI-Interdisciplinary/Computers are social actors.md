# Computers are social actors

Computers are social actors (CASA) is a paradigm which states that humans mindlessly apply the same social heuristics used for human interactions to computers, because they call to mind similar social attributes as humans. CASA is considered an application of the Media Equation Theory within the context of human-computer interaction.

# The South African Library for the Blind Act, 1998 (Act No. 91 of 1998)

The South African Library for the Blind Act, 1998 (Act No. 91 of 1998) is an Act of the Parliament of South Africa that established the South African Library for the Blind as a national public entity responsible for providing library and information services to blind and print-handicapped readers. The Act was assented to on 20 October 1998 and came into operation on 1 April 2000.
The Act provides a legislative framework for the delivery of specialised library services and accessible information formats, including braille and audio materials, to persons who are unable to read conventional print due to visual or other print disabilities.

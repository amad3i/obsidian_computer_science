# Fleksy

Fleksy was a proprietary third-party virtual keyboard developed by ThingThing Ltd. for Android and iOS devices. The keyboard used error-correcting algorithms that analyze the touch region on the keyboard and pass input through a language model to determine the intended word. The application also supported swipe gestures for functions such as inserting spaces, deleting text, and cycling through word suggestions.

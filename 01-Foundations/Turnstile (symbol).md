# Turnstile (symbol)

In mathematical logic and computer science the symbol ⊢ (
  
    
      
        ⊢
      
    
    
  
) has taken the name turnstile because of its resemblance to a typical turnstile. It is also referred to as tee and is often read as "yields", "proves", "satisfies" or "entails".

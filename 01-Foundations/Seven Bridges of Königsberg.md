# Seven Bridges of Königsberg

The Seven Bridges of Königsberg is a historical puzzle asking for a walking tour through the bridges of the city of Königsberg (now Kaliningrad) that is a circuit ending where it started. Its mathematical formalization and proof of impossibility by Leonhard Euler, in 1736, laid the foundations of graph theory and foreshadowed the idea of topology.

# Pulse shaping

In electronics and telecommunications, pulse shaping is the process of changing a transmitted pulse's waveform to optimize the signal for its intended purpose or communication channel. This is often done by limiting the bandwidth of the transmission and filtering the pulses to control intersymbol interference (ISI). Pulse shaping is particularly important in radio frequency (RF) communication for fitting the signal within a certain frequency band and is typically applied after line coding and before modulation.

# Support (mathematics)

In mathematics, the support of a real-valued function 
  
    
      
        f
      
    
    
  
 is the subset of the function's domain consisting of those elements that are not mapped to zero. If the domain of 
  
    
      
        f
      
    
    
  
 is a topological space, then the support of 
  
    
      
        f
      
    
    
  
 is instead defined as the smallest closed set containing all points not mapped to zero. This concept is used widely in mathematical analysis.

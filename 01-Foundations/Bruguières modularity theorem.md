# Bruguières modularity theorem

In mathematics, the Bruguières modularity theorem is a theorem about modular tensor categories. It asserts that two different formulations of the modularity condition of a modular tensor category are equivalent. The Bruguières modularity theorem was introduced by mathematician Alain Bruguières in the year 2000. The first notion of modularity used in the theorem statement is in terms of the non-degeneracy of the braid statistics of the simple objects, and the other is in terms of the non-degeneracy of the modular S-matrix.

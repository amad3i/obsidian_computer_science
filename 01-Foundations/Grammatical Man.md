# Grammatical Man

Grammatical Man: Information, Entropy, Language, and Life is a 1982 book written by Jeremy Campbell, then Washington correspondent for the Evening Standard. The book examines the topics of probability, information theory, cybernetics, genetics, and linguistics. 
Information processes are used to frame and examine all of existence, from the Big Bang to DNA to human communication to artificial intelligence.

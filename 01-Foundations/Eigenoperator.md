# Eigenoperator

In mathematics, an eigenoperator, A, of a matrix H is a linear operator such that

  
    
      
        H
        ,
        A
        =
        λ
        A
        
      
    
    
  

where 
  
    
      
        λ
      
    
    
  
 is a corresponding scalar called an eigenvalue.

# Dickson's lemma

In mathematics, Dickson's lemma states that every set of 
  
    
      
        n
      
    
    
  
-tuples of natural numbers has finitely many minimal elements. This simple fact from combinatorics has become attributed to the American algebraist L. E. Dickson, who used it to prove a result in number theory about perfect numbers. However, the lemma was certainly known earlier, for example to Paul Gordan in his research on invariant theory.

# Overcategory

In mathematics, an overcategory (also called a slice category) is a construction from category theory used in multiple contexts, such as with covering spaces (espace étalé). They were introduced as a mechanism for keeping track of data surrounding a fixed object

 in some category

. The dual notion is that of an undercategory (also called a coslice category).
Both can be expressed in terms of the more general construction of a comma category.

# Cyclic number

A cyclic number is an integer for which cyclic permutations of the digits are successive integer multiples of the number.  The most widely-known cyclic number is 142857, whose first six integer multiples are

142857 × 1 = 142857
142857 × 2 = 285714
142857 × 3 = 428571
142857 × 4 = 571428
142857 × 5 = 714285
142857 × 6 = 857142

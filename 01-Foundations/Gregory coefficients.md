Gregory coefficients are alternating Gn = (−1)n−1|Gn| for n > 0
 and decreasing in absolute value. These numbers are named after James Gregory who introduced them in 1670 in the numerical integration context. They were subsequently rediscovered by many mathematicians and often appear in works of modern authors, who do not always recognize them.

# Proofs of Fermat's little theorem

This article collects together a variety of proofs of Fermat's little theorem, which states that

  
    
      
        
          a
          
            p
          
        
        ≡
        a
        
          
          mod
          
          p
        
      
    
    
  

for every prime number p and every integer a (see modular arithmetic).

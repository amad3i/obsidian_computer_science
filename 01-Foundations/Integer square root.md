# Integer square root

In number theory, the integer square root (isqrt) of a non-negative integer n is the non-negative integer m which is the greatest integer less than or equal to the square root of n,

  
    
      
        isqrt
         
        n
        =
        ⌊
        
          
            n
          
        
        ⌋
        .
      
    
    
  

For example, 
  
    
      
        isqrt
         
        27
        =
        ⌊
        
          
            27
          
        
        ⌋
        ⌊
        5.19615242270663...
        ⌋
        5.

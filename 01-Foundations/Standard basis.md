Standard bases can be defined for other vector spaces, whose definition involves coefficients, such as polynomials and matrices.  In both cases, the standard basis consists of the elements of the space such that all coefficients but one are 0 and the non-zero one is 1. For polynomials, the standard basis thus consists of the monomials and is commonly called monomial basis.

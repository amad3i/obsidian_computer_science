# Gambling and information theory

Statistical inference might be thought of as gambling theory applied to real-world events. The myriad applications for logarithmic information measures inform optimal decisions when given partial information.  In that sense, information theory can be considered a formal expression of the theory of gambling, since they are games of chance.

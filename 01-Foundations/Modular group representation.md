# Modular group representation

In mathematics, the modular group representation (or simply modular representation) of a modular tensor category 
  
    
      
        
          
            C
          
        
      
    
    
  
 is a representation of the modular group 
  
    
      
        
          
            SL
          
          
            2
          
        
        
          Z
        
      
    
    
  
 associated to 
  
    
      
        
          
            C
          
        
      
    
    
  
. It is from the existence of the modular representation that modular tensor categories get their name.
From the perspective of topological quantum field theory, the modular representation of 
  
    
      
        
          
            C
          
        
      
    
    
  
 arrises naturally as the representation of the mapping class group of the torus associated to the Reshetikhin–Turaev topological quantum field theory associated to 
  
    
      
        
          
            C
          
        
      
    
    
  
. As such, modular tensor categories can be used to define projective representations of the mapping class groups of all closed surfaces.

# Nscale

Nscale is a Europe-based technology company specializing in AI-ready data centers and high-performance GPU cloud infrastructure. Headquartered in London, the company was founded in 2024 by Joshua Payne and Nathan Townsend.
As a full-stack hyperscaler, the company delivers infrastructure to support the compute demands of artificial intelligence, machine learning and high-performance computing projects.

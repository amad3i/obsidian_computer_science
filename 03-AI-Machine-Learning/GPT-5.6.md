# GPT-5.6

GPT-5.6 (Generative Pre-trained Transformer 5.6) is a large language model (LLM) developed by OpenAI and released on July 9, 2026. It is a family of models that comes in three distinct variants, ranked from least to most capable: Luna, Terra, and Sol. The models were designed to expand user capabilities across enterprise work, coding, scientific research, and cybersecurity.
Due to government restrictions levied by the Donald Trump administration, GPT-5.6 was first released as a limited preview only on June 26, 2026 for "small group of trusted partners", despite the planned public rollout in the same month.

# STIT logic

STIT logic (from seeing to it that) is a family of modal and branching-time logics for reasoning about agency and choice. A typical STIT operator has the form 
  
    
      
        i
         
        
          
            s
            t
            i
            t
          
        
        :
        φ
      
    
    
  
, usually read as "agent 
  
    
      
        i
      
    
    
  
 sees to it that 
  
    
      
        φ
      
    
    
  
", and is interpreted in models where agents choose between alternative possible futures.
STIT logics are used in action theory, deontic logic, epistemic logic, and the theory of intelligent agents to formalise notions such as "could have done otherwise", responsibility, joint action, and strategic ability in an indeterministic world.

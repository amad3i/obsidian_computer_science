# AI agent

In the context of generative artificial intelligence, AI agents (also referred to as compound AI systems, agentic AI, or AI tools) are a class of intelligent agents that can pursue goals, use tools, and take actions with varying degrees of autonomy. In practice, they usually operate within human-defined objectives, constraints, and available tools.
